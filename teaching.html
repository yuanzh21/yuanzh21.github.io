UFUG 2103: Linear Algebra

Instructor: Zhenyi Yuan
Proposed offering: Spring 2026/27

Linear algebra provides a mathematical foundation for engineering, science, data analysis, optimization, control, and machine learning. This course introduces its central concepts and develops both computational skills and geometric intuition.

Course Topics

  • Systems of linear equations and matrix operations
  • Vector spaces, linear independence, bases, and dimension
  • Linear transformations and matrix representations
  • Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization
  • Inner products, orthogonality, and least-squares problems
  • Selected applications of linear algebra

IOTA 5206: Interplay Between Artificial Intelligence and Control Systems

Instructor: Zhenyi Yuan
Proposed offering: Spring 2026/27
Credits: 3

This proposed postgraduate course develops a foundational understanding of artificial intelligence and control systems, with an emphasis on the interplay between machine learning, dynamical systems, and feedback control. Students will study how learning-based methods support system modeling, prediction, planning, decision-making, and controller design, as well as how control theory contributes to the analysis, interpretability, safety, robustness, adaptation, and reliable operation of intelligent systems.

Course Topics

  • Machine learning fundamentals and deep neural network training
  • Dynamical systems, stability, and feedback control
  • Attention, transformers, and large language models
  • Safety-critical control and model predictive control
  • Control-theoretic tools for modern AI systems
  • AI-enabled intelligent control systems
